GOOGLE DORKS LIST
=================

Ready-to-use dorks, grouped by what they find.
Last checked: November 2026.

Replace example.com with your own domain, or one you have
permission to test. Running searches is passive, but what you
do with the results isn't. Stick to what you're allowed to look at.
